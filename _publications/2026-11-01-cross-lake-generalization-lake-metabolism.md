Lake gross primary production (GPP), respiration (R) and net ecosystem production (NEP) are key indicators of lake ecosystem function, and satellite-driven machine learning models provide a scalable way to estimate them. Such models are typically trained on lakes with in-situ metabolism measurements, yet their ability to be transferred to other lakes remains uncertain. This study assesses the cross-lake generalization of satellite-driven machine learning models for lake metabolism estimation, providing a basis for reliable, large-scale application of these models.
